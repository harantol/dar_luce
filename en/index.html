The house

A place designed to feel like home

Dar Luce is a country house near Essaouira, set in the middle of a landscaped garden of more than one hectare. Bright and easy to live in, it is laid out around four independent double bedrooms, all opening onto a large terrace with a pool.

The garden has a simple, authentic beauty: lush planting and three large cascading ponds, home to frogs, goldfish, terrapins and water lilies. The whole property is at guests' disposal during their stay.
